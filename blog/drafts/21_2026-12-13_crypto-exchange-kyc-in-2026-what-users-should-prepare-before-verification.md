## Verify the entity before your identity

Read the legal entity in the terms and confirm that it serves your country. EEA residents may be directed to WhiteBIT EU rather than the global platform. The entity determines the applicable privacy notice, regulator, product set, and complaints route.

Avoid search ads and look-alike domains. Start from a saved official URL or the disclosed partner link.

## Prepare the documents

Common requirements include a valid passport, national ID, or residence permit; a selfie or liveness check; residential address; and sometimes proof of address. Higher tiers or certain payment providers can require additional questionnaires.

Use current, unedited documents. Make sure the legal name, birth date, and address match the form. Blurry images and inconsistent transliteration are common reasons for delay.

## Understand verification levels

Some exchanges use levels that unlock different services. WhiteBIT's current guidance describes tiered access and different EU treatment. Do not automatically provide the maximum set of documents when the required level for your intended use is lower—but understand that deposits, withdrawals, fiat routes, or cards may require more information.

Choose based on the feature you need, not the desire to make every menu item available.

## Protect the verification session

Use a patched personal device, private environment, and secure network. Close screen-sharing tools. Do not let another person control the browser or camera. After verification, remove document photos from temporary folders and shared photo libraries where practical.

The exchange may need identity data; a stranger in a messaging app does not.

## Source of funds and financial questionnaires

Large or unusual transactions can trigger additional review. Keep bank statements, payslips, invoices, sale records, and transaction history that explain legitimate funds. Answer accurately and consistently.

This is not a technical obstacle to “bypass.” It is part of anti-money-laundering controls. If the request is unclear, contact official support through the authenticated platform.

## Privacy questions worth asking

Read what data is collected, why, how long it is retained, which processors receive it, and how rights requests work. Check whether the platform describes GDPR handling for EU users. Store your own copy of the accepted terms and the date.

No online identity repository is risk-free. Limit verified accounts to services with a real role in your workflow.

## What KYC does not guarantee

Verification does not make a token safe, guarantee withdrawals, insure the portfolio, or eliminate phishing. It can support compliance and account recovery, but exchange and market risk remain.

After approval, enable passkey or app-based 2FA, anti-phishing, session controls, and withdrawal restrictions before funding.
