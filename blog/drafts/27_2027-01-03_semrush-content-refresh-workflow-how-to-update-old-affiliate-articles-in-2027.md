## Build the candidate list

Look for pages with falling clicks, ranking decline, impressions without clicks, positions 4–20, keyword cannibalization, outdated year references, or broken commercial links. Combine Semrush visibility data with Search Console and revenue data.

Traffic alone is not priority. Weight business relevance, conversion rate, factual risk, and how realistic improvement is.

## Diagnose before editing

Classify the problem: intent changed, competitors provide better structure, facts are stale, title earns poor click-through, internal links are weak, the page is cannibalized, or the offer no longer converts. Different problems require different edits.

Do not add 1,000 words because the page lost rankings. More text can dilute the answer.

## Refresh the evidence

For product and financial pages, re-check pricing, availability, legal entity, feature names, security controls, and restrictions on official sources. Replace vague “best” claims with criteria and evidence. Date material changes.

Update affiliate disclosures and links through the canonical registry. Remove placeholders, redirects you do not control, and references to discontinued offers.

## Align with current intent

Review the current results for the primary query. Determine whether users want a review, comparison, tutorial, price answer, or shortlist. Rebuild the opening and headings around that job while keeping sections that still satisfy it.

Answer the main decision early. Affiliate articles that hide the verdict behind a long introduction lose trust and clicks.

## Improve internal architecture

Link from relevant high-authority pages to the refreshed article with natural anchor text. Add contextual links from the refreshed page to setup, fees, security, and comparison content. Avoid orphan clusters where every article repeats the same background.

The content hub should move readers from broad comparison to product decision to safe use.

## Publish without resetting the asset

Keep the URL when intent and topic remain the same. Update the title and date only when the page is materially refreshed. Preserve useful backlinks, redirects, and structured data. Record the exact change date and baseline metrics.

If two pages compete for the same intent, consolidate carefully and redirect the weaker URL.

## Measure the refresh

Track crawl and index status, ranking distribution, impressions, clicks, click-through rate, conversions, and revenue. Compare against seasonality and site-wide changes. Give search engines time to process the page before editing it again.

Document what changed so future editors can distinguish a failed hypothesis from an incomplete rollout.

## FAQ

### How often should affiliate articles be refreshed?

Refresh when facts, intent, performance, or offers change. High-risk financial claims may need more frequent review than evergreen tutorials.

### Should the publication date be changed?

Only after a material update. Cosmetic edits should not be presented as a new review.
