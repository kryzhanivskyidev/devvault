## Fees: look beyond the headline percentage

WhiteBIT's live fee page currently shows standard spot maker and taker fees, while individual pairs and VIP levels can differ. The trading fee is only one component. Your real cost is the sum of deposit charges, trading fee, spread, slippage, withdrawal fee, network choice, and any bank or card conversion cost.

For an occasional buyer, a slightly higher trading fee can matter less than an expensive card deposit or a poorly chosen withdrawal network. For a frequent trader, small maker/taker differences compound quickly. Build the comparison around your own route—EUR in, chosen asset bought, asset out—rather than copying one attractive number from an exchange table.

## Security controls worth switching on immediately

WhiteBIT documents app-based 2FA or passkeys, anti-phishing codes, address management, automatic logout, and device/session management. Use them. A strong exchange password is not enough when the associated email account, SIM, or recovery channel is weak.

Create a unique password with a password manager, secure the email account with its own phishing-resistant authentication, store recovery material offline, and whitelist withdrawal addresses where practical. Never approve a login or withdrawal because a person in Telegram or a direct message claims to be support. Real support does not need your seed phrase, private key, or authenticator backup.
