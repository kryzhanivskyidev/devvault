## 1–3: entity, eligibility, and verification

**1. Legal entity:** Identify the company in the terms. EEA users may be served through a dedicated EU entity rather than a global platform.

**2. Regulatory status:** Check the relevant regulator or official register. MiCA authorization is meaningful, but it does not insure assets or remove market risk.

**3. KYC path:** Review required documents, age limits, restricted countries, and which features require additional verification. Submit documents only through the official domain.

## 4–6: money in, trading, and total cost

**4. Fiat funding:** Confirm your currency, bank/card route, minimum, processing time, account-name rules, and provider fees.

**5. Liquidity:** Inspect the actual order book and volume for the pairs you need. A long asset list is useless when your pair is thin.

**6. Total cost:** Add funding, trading fee, spread, slippage, withdrawal, and network or bank costs. Compare one identical scenario across platforms.

## 7–9: security, custody, and withdrawals

**7. Account controls:** Look for passkeys or authenticator 2FA, anti-phishing, session/device management, and address allowlisting.

**8. Custody model:** Understand that the exchange controls private keys while funds remain on-platform. Decide a maximum balance.

**9. Withdrawal route:** Check supported networks, fees, minimums, memo/tag requirements, address books, and current system status. Test the route before using size.

## 10–12: recovery, records, and exit

**10. Support and recovery:** Find official support before a crisis. Understand what happens after a lost second factor or locked account.

**11. Records:** Confirm transaction history, downloadable statements, timestamps, fees, and identifiers are sufficient for tax and accounting.

**12. Exit:** Know how to withdraw assets, export data, revoke API keys, close sessions, and close the account. An invisible exit is a hidden cost.

## Red flags that end the evaluation

Walk away from guaranteed returns, pressure to deposit immediately, support agents asking for seed phrases or remote access, unclear legal entities, copycat domains, deposits routed through personal accounts, or an exchange that makes withdrawal terms difficult to find.

Also distrust reviews that never mention country variation, custody, or how to leave. An affiliate relationship should be disclosed and should not turn risk into a footnote.

## FAQ

### Does MiCA make an exchange safe?

MiCA creates regulatory requirements for authorized providers; it does not eliminate hacks, operational failures, token losses, or user mistakes.

### Should I complete KYC on several exchanges?

Only when each account has a defined role. More verified accounts mean more sensitive data and security maintenance.
